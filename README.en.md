## About

An economics student who writes code. I am interested in the full product development cycle: from framing the problem and architecture through the interface and deployment. I keep drifting toward the seam where software meets economics: unit economics, pricing, product metrics.

What I am doing now:

- Building **workdo.ru**, a SaaS platform. I am the founder of the project.
- Leading the **StartUp Space VGTU** branch, the university entrepreneurship community. Since 27 May 2026 I help students turn ideas into working projects.
- Assembling a portfolio of hackathons, competitions and research work.

Principles I work by:

- Understand the whole problem before writing code. The last three hackathons I reached the finals or won came down to exactly this.
- Do not reach for a framework before understanding what it does under the hood. [iaai](https://github.com/glcskl/iaai) is a neural network built from scratch in pure NumPy, no `fit()`, for that reason.
- Ship things that work. Every hackathon repository has a README with working setup instructions.
